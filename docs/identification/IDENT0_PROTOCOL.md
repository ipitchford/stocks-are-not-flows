# IDENT-0 protocol

IDENT-0 asks a deliberately prior question: if the planned observation schedule
were generated by a transparent local-response system, could the focal top and
middle resource amplitudes be recovered without assigning rival mechanisms to
the top-resource block?

## Primitive blocks

Estimated core blocks:

1. top non-housing resource flow;
2. middle resource flow;
3. mortgage/credit conditions;
4. primitive planning or structure-supply conditions;
5. landlord tax/transaction wedge;
6. safe/mortgage rate path; and
7. demographics.

Adversarial omitted-DGP blocks:

1. non-rich social-comparison demand;
2. institutional rental-supply entry and household-landlord crowd-out;
3. rental-owner segmentation or rental efficiency; and
4. planning/permit restriction distinct from an endogenous housing-stock change.

## Observation boundary

The public core may observe household tenure, aggregate PRS dwelling stock,
aggregate HPI, rents or stock-rent indices, net additions, transaction turnover,
mortgage prices, and Great Britain non-housing wealth moments at their actual
frequencies. Gross conversion flows and landlord identity are absent.

The internal panel is a deviation system. Hash-pinned adapters preserve the
frozen public units: tenure targets are percentage levels and `T07_RENT_END` is
nominal pounds per week using `R_model = R0 * exp(log_response / 100)`. The
configured baseline levels and diagonal Gaussian scales are synthetic recovery
design inputs, not English estimates or administrative-data standard errors.

`H05_PRICE_RENT_INDEX` remains non-modelled because it is frozen at 60 monthly
dates and the annual state system has no preregistered interpolation operator.
The previously drafted unregistered PRS-dwelling diagnostic is not in the
frozen schedule.

## Signed closure receipts

Amendment 02 resolves the signed truth design with D1 injection, D1R top
withdrawal, D2 middle loss, and D2R middle injection elementary receipts. Joint
open-economy cases preserve their children in a C0 composite; the all-zero
anchor uses N0. Only the registered positive-top/equal-middle-loss direction is
direct E0 redistribution. A nonzero-sum composite is never called budget
neutral.

## Required audits

- explicit resource and per-recipient units;
- labelled D1 injection, D2 loss, or E0 redistribution closure;
- executable stock-flow and wealth-share measurement;
- three-step finite-difference Jacobians;
- singular values, numerical rank, condition number, and column angles;
- known-truth noiseless and noisy recovery;
- multi-start objective geometry;
- objective profiles and null false attribution;
- adversarial rival-only DGPs;
- missing-observation schedule;
- materiality and bound-width interpretation; and
- mutation controls under ordinary and optimized Python.

The complete preregistered design contains 313 truth cases and 6,573 fits. Unit
tests run only deterministic small designs. A future full-gate command must be
explicit and must write its manifest before computation; the present CLI stops
at the fail-fast front.

The current command executes a fail-fast front: accounting and adapters, the
64-point local audit, collinearity controls, exact-mimic detection, and bounded
41-point profiles for the four deterministic omitted-mechanism rivals. It can
return `FAIL-IDENT-0` or `CONTINUE-TO-FULL-GATE`; it cannot return a pass. On a
blocking failure it records every unrun full-gate criterion instead of spending
210,336 optimizer starts.

## Decisions

- `PASS-IDENT-0`: authorize MIN-EXEC construction only.
- `FAIL-IDENT-0`: prohibit unique historical attribution; retain scenarios,
  identified sets, or a credible near-zero bound.

The local-response matrix still emits aggregate HPI, tenure, and age-25–44
outcomes directly. Until executable sector-price, dwelling-to-household, and
lifecycle observation maps exist, those omissions are explicit complete-gate
blockers even if the numerical rank front passes.

The gate repeats on MIN-EXEC and on the final long-duration-mortgage model.
