# Final novelty and publication assessment

**Assessment date:** 2026-08-01  
**Recommended title:** *Stocks Are Not Flows: An Identification Audit of Investor
Pressure and Tenure Change in England*  
**Decision:** `PRACTICABLE-AS-IDENTIFICATION-AUDIT + COLLISION-ON-BROAD-MECHANISM + STOP-UNIQUE + STOP-CAUSE`

## Bottom line

The project is practicable as a sophisticated identification and measurement
paper. It is not practicable, with the evidence presently assembled, as a paper
claiming that rising top wealth caused England's 2008–2019 tenure change or as a
structural decomposition assigning a unique historical contribution to that
channel.

The novelty gate materially changed the project. The broad mechanism, the main
qualitative sign pattern, endogenous rent/own/landlord architecture, and linked
property-flow measurement all have close antecedents. The defensible residual is
the transparent demonstration that tenure stocks, gross property-use flows, and
purchaser-resource contributions are different estimands; the fail-fast audit of
their distinguishability; and the exact condition under which a new measurement
would break the observed focal–rival equivalence.

## Collision findings

1. **Broad inequality-to-housing signs are occupied.** Head, Lloyd-Ellis and
   Stacey establish an equilibrium setting in which more inequality lowers
   homeownership and raises the average price-rent ratio. The paper cannot claim
   a new joint sign pattern.
2. **Rich portfolio pressure is occupied.** Fudong Zhang's 2015/16 paper models
   a resource shift toward wealthy households, portfolio reallocation into
   housing, higher house prices, a lower rent-price return, and delayed entry by
   low-resource households. The paper cannot claim the first such mechanism.
3. **Tenure and landlord architecture is occupied.** Close UK and European models
   already contain endogenous renting, ownership, household landlords,
   institutional investors, credit constraints, and supply margins. Model
   integration alone is not novelty.
4. **Property flows have already been linked.** Bracke links Land Registry sales
   to subsequent rental listings using a six-month rule. The Bank of England's
   later four-year classification distinguishes owner-to-landlord,
   landlord-to-owner, and inter-landlord flows. No first-flow or wholly-unmeasured
   claim is permitted.
5. **Those antecedents do not observe the proposed forcing variable.** Flow
   classifications remain sensitive to coverage, lag, revision, and
   classification; they do not directly observe purchaser top non-housing
   resources or identify a wealth-concentration shock.

This is a bounded review, not proof of worldwide priority. The safe wording is
that the review did not locate a public source jointly observing seller tenure,
purchaser landlord status, and purchaser top non-housing resources.

The claim-level evidence, versions, page anchors, and decision consequences are
bound in `DECISIVE_SOURCE_LEDGER.md`; the full bounded search record is retained
in the frozen Stage 0 novelty-gate release.

## Residual contribution that survives

The manuscript makes five connected contributions without relying on a priority
claim:

1. a stock/flow/contribution ontology with explicit shock closure;
2. a prospective 57-moment fail-fast gate that distinguishes local rank from
   mechanism specificity;
3. an authoritative stopped result, including known-negative and deliberately
   adversarial controls, that prevents an expensive model from being mistaken for
   identification;
4. a source- and manifest-bound public accounting layer with a deterministic
   offline replay; and
5. a formal discriminator, conditional sharp-set framework, and measurement-value
   criterion that convert the stopped model into a minimum-data agenda.

The central housing result is negative but informative: within the registered
synthetic design, the focal response is in the admitted nuisance span in both
public and scaled units. Local full rank of the restricted seven-column matrix
does not rescue unique attribution after the rival columns are admitted.

## Evidence-backed claims

The following claims are currently supported:

- the authoritative IDENT-0 run returned `FAIL-IDENT-0` and stopped MIN-EXEC;
- all 57 targets and 313 closure cases were covered, and 64/64 maintained local
  rank points passed;
- three of four deliberately constructed rival profiles excluded zero, which is
  a harness warning and **not** a population false-positive rate;
- seven official public objects passed exact hash and schema checks, and all 43
  registered Gate A checks passed;
- the owner point estimate for English households with an HRP aged 25–44 fell
  11.9487 percentage points from 2008–09 to 2018–19 while the private-renter point
  estimate rose 12.3269 points;
- the online and offline Gate A runs have byte-identical raw files, nine derived
  CSVs, six figures, and table metadata, while their provenance manifests differ
  by design; and
- the synthetic focal response has relative projection residuals
  `1.46097e-14` and `2.32265e-14`, below the fixed `2e-10` span tolerance, so no
  dual discriminator exists in the admitted design.

## Forbidden or blocked claims

Do not state or imply that:

- top wealth concentration caused the English tenure path;
- the design estimates a historical top-resource contribution;
- 75% is an empirical or population false-positive rate;
- a normalized HPI/PIPR index is a gross or net rental yield;
- household tenure, dwelling stock, and gross property transitions share a
  denominator or estimand;
- a cash buyer, former owner, company owner, BTL mortgagor, and landlord are
  interchangeable categories;
- deterministic replay is independent replication;
- hashes establish source validity or economic truth; or
- the broad mechanism, landlord model, or owner-to-landlord flow concept is new.

## Publication-standard judgment

The current package is a serious anonymous submission candidate. It has a
coherent research question, calibrated novelty boundary, formal results, exact
empirical accounting, adversarial controls, machine-readable provenance,
reproducible code, a tested bibliography, and a compiled 30-page manuscript.
Its likely editorial success depends on presenting structural non-identification
as the result rather than apologising for the absence of a causal estimate.

It should be circulated first as a candidate/preprint to a housing economist and
an econometrician with partial-identification expertise. A regular empirical
housing-economics article would require a separately reviewed, source-backed
response set or a new linked design that satisfies the measurement criterion.
Adding restricted data without breaking the focal–rival equivalence would not
upgrade the claim.

## Human and external work before journal submission

1. Named authors must approve the manuscript and complete affiliations,
   acknowledgements, funding, conflicts, and author-contribution fields.
2. A domain expert should check the interpretation of Stevenson, Zhang, the Bank
   flow measure, and every official-series methodology note line by line.
3. An independent party should reproduce the release from fresh extraction; the
   present offline replay is deterministic self-reproduction, not independence.
4. The target journal's style, word limit, data policy, anonymisation rules, and
   AI-assistance disclosure must be applied.
5. The declarations must be finalized, and all URLs/DOIs should receive a final
   pre-submission click-through audit.
6. For the v1.0.0 public discussion preprint, the original code, manuscript,
   documentation, and original figures are explicitly dedicated under CC0 1.0.
   The official raw data retain their publisher Open Government Licence terms.

Until those human and external steps occur, label the artifact **public
discussion preprint** or **publication candidate**, not peer reviewed,
independently replicated, or publication ready in the editorial sense.
