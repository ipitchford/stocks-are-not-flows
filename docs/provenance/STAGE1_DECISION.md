## Novelty boundary

The broad mechanism in which a poor-to-rich resource shift increases wealthy
housing investment, raises house prices, lowers rent-price returns, and delays
entry is prior art, including Fudong Zhang (2015/16). Endogenous tenure,
landlord choice, UK structural housing models, wealth-in-utility, and rich saving
motives also have close antecedents.

The residual contribution is now an identification and measurement audit:

> Distinguish tenure stocks, gross property-use flows, and mechanism
> contributions; show what the registered aggregate observation map can and
> cannot identify; and quantify a contribution only if independently
> source-backed response sets support a defensible sharp set.

The authoritative v2 run returned `FAIL-IDENT-0`. The maintained seven-block
matrix passed its local-rank front, but three of four deliberately constructed
omitted-mechanism cases induced a nonzero focal profile and essential public
observation maps remained absent. This is a designed specificity warning, not
a population false-positive rate. MIN-EXEC and unique historical attribution
are stopped.

The subsequent public accounting run reproduced seven hash-pinned official
sources and all 43 registered validation checks. Its bounded success label is
`OPEN-SUBSET-REPRODUCED+FULL-GATE-A-HELD`; a fresh offline replay reproduced the
raw, derived, and figure directories byte for byte. The design-geometry
certificate then showed the synthetic focal response in the admitted nuisance
span at both public-unit and preregistered scaled representations. Those results
support publication of an identification audit and a conditional sharp-set
framework. They do not supply an empirical England contribution interval.

## Evidence ceiling

- No causal top-wealth-to-tenure claim.
- No unique historical attribution before final-model synthetic identification.
- The unrestricted open core does not directly label gross property-use flows
  or purchaser resources. Prior transaction-listing work has measured partial
  owner/landlord flow concepts, so no first-flow or wholly-unmeasured claim is
  permitted.
- No general-equilibrium claim for IDENT-0.
- No assumption that a solver-selected root is unique.
