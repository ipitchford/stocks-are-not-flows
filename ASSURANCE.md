# Assurance boundary

**Status:** anonymous public discussion preprint v1.0.0; unrefereed; not
independently reproduced; not a causal estimate.

- source-level novelty and nearest-neighbour review with a calibrated residual
  claim;

- seven official public-source objects and 43 registered Gate A checks;
- hash verification for authoritative report files and redistributed raw inputs;
- deterministic replay of the registered public-data transformations;
- negative, sign-reversing, and adversarial design controls;
- LaTeX compilation, reference resolution, PDF structural checks, and release
  privacy scans.

## What the evidence establishes

The public accounting layer reproduces specified English tenure, price, rent,
supply, and UK mortgage-rate paths. In the registered synthetic response design,
the focal top-resource response lies in the admitted rival-and-nuisance span, so
the design supplies no unique linear discriminator. The planned structural fit
was therefore stopped.

## What it does not establish

- that wealthy saving caused England's tenure shift or greater poverty;
- that wealthy investors had no effect;
- a causal coefficient or historical contribution share;
- an empirical England sharp interval;
- novelty of the broad investor-pressure mechanism;
- independent reproduction, expert acceptance, formal verification, or peer
  review.

The most valuable next measurement jointly observes gross property-use
conversion, purchaser landlord status, and pre-purchase non-housing resources.
Externally justified response bounds would then permit an empirical sharp set.
