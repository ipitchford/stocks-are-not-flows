# v1.0.0 — public discussion preprint

This release publishes the stopped identification audit as a positive
measurement result.

## Central finding

England's public aggregate housing series document a substantial shift from
owner occupation to private renting, but the registered observation map cannot
uniquely attribute any share of that shift to rising top resources. The result
does not establish either a positive or a zero causal effect.
